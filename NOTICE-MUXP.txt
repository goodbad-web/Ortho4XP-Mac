MUXP engine attribution
=======================

Ortho4XP embeds the headless MUXP mesh-update engine based on the
muxp-mac repository at commit 251d09e036f3b24d46c87da33119f5b05e1ea326.

Upstream project: https://github.com/nofaceinbook/muxp/
Upstream license: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 or later (LGPL-3.0-or-later)

The vendored files under src/muxp_engine/ retain their upstream copyright and
license notices. Changes made for this integration are limited to package
imports, the headless application boundary, and preservation of unknown DSF
atoms during a read/modify/write cycle.
